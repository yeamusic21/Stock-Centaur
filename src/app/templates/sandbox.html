How the projections work

The model

A gradient-boosted tree model predicts how much each stock will beat or trail the typical stock over the next 21 trading days (about 30 calendar days); the market's average return is then added back. Its inputs are the stock's price slopes over the last 1 year, 6 months, 3 months, 1 month, 2 weeks and 3 days, how volatile it was over the last 1 and 3 months, its worst drop over the past year and how far it sits below its 52-week high. Two more tree models predict the 10th and 90th percentile of the return, and that range is widened just enough to contain 80% of outcomes on held-out data.

The portfolio range

The sandbox runs a Monte Carlo simulation: it draws 10,000 possible 30-day outcomes for every stock from its predicted return and range, correlated using how the stocks moved together over the past year, then adds up the portfolio's value in each one. The projected value is the median outcome.
