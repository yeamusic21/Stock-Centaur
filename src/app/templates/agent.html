Research Agent

Pick a portfolio and the agent pulls fresh prices and news, runs the price model through 10,000 simulations, and has your local model write a review.

How the agent works

  1. Score. Every holding gets the price model's 30-day forecast. Tickers outside the trend rankings (ETFs, funds) are downloaded and scored on the spot.
  2. Pull prices. A fresh year of daily prices from Yahoo gives recent returns, moving averages, volatility and drawdowns.
  3. Simulate. The same 10,000-run Monte Carlo as the Portfolio Sandbox gives the portfolio's likely range and chance of a loss.
  4. Read the news. Headlines come from the free Yahoo Finance and Google News RSS feeds; the largest holdings' top articles are scraped for their text.
  5. Write. Everything is handed to the model you picked in Connections, running in LM Studio on this computer. Nothing is sent to a paid service.

Detailed mode first writes a brief for each of the 8 largest holdings, then the review from those briefs. It reads more carefully but makes up to 9 model calls instead of 1.

Reports are saved in data/stock_centaur.json. Nothing here is investment advice.
